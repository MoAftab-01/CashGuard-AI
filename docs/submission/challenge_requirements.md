# Verified challenge requirements

Checked October 18, 2026 using the official challenge page, its submission template,
contest-specific rules, general rules and DevRelay's challenge details.

- Challenge: [Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 2: Side Hustle](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week2-2026-10-12).
- Entry window: October 12 at noon PDT through October 18 at 11:59 PM PDT.
- Deadline: **2026-10-19 06:59 UTC / October 19, 2026 at 12:29 PM IST**.
- Required: a published DEV template entry, `devchallenge` and `hf26challenge` tags,
  a new project built in the entry window, a deployed demo or video, a code repository
  link, plausible Side Hustle value, and an explanation of open innovation.
- One entry per participant for this challenge. Writing is weighted most heavily;
  relevance, creativity, execution and meaningful partner use also matter.
- Only **Best Use of Gemma** is proposed. The project uses installed `gemma3:4b`
  through local Ollama; no other partner category is claimed. Category fit does not
  establish personal eligibility or guarantee a prize.
- A first user and agent-session embed are optional. Neither is invented here.
- On October 18, 2026, the owner confirmed that the project genuinely began in the
  entry window and that personal eligibility under the official rules was checked.
  This is owner attestation, not independent verification or an organizer decision.
  Local file dates and test history do not prove eligibility; do not rewrite history.
- Changes committed after the deadline must be identified honestly in the README.
